J3: "disposal of scrap requires approval and produces a disposal record, **so that write-offs are controlled and auditable**." This is the auditable half, and what an auditor actually asks for is narrow: what was destroyed, who authorised it, how it was got rid of, and who took it away. So the approval trail is not an appendix here — it is the point of the document, and it prints even when the disposal was auto-approved, because "nobody was asked and here is why" is itself the answer to the question. The value is shown only when the tenant tracks money (C8).
